# My Favorite Series: The Penthouse

## Overview
**The Penthouse: War in Life** is a *Korean drama* filled with mystery, revenge, and drama.  
It tells the story of wealthy families living in Hera Palace, where ambition and secrets rule their lives.

---

## Main Characters
1. **Shim Su-ryeon** – A kind and elegant woman seeking justice for her family.  
2. **Cheon Seo-jin** – A talented but greedy woman obsessed with success.  
3. **Oh Yoon-hee** – A hardworking mother who will do anything for her daughter.  
4. **Joo Dan-tae** – A powerful businessman hiding dark secrets.

---

## Why I Love It
- It has an *intense storyline* that keeps me excited every episode.  
- The acting is **amazing** and full of emotion.  
- Every twist and turn makes the drama unpredictable!
